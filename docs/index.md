# R15 Wildlife Monitoring Rover
 
Welcome to the **R15 Wildlife Monitoring Rover User and Safety Manual**.
This document provides detailed guidance on the setup, operation, and maintenance of the R15 mobile robotic platform, a compact, remotely operated system designed for non-invasive wildlife observation in natural habitats.

The R15 rover was developed to support ecological research and conservation by enabling quiet, camouflaged, and low-impact monitoring of animals in environments that are often inaccessible or disruptive to humans. Through a combination of teleoperation, modular design, and real-time video streaming, the system allows users to observe and record wildlife activity safely and effectively.

This manual assumes that the reader has a technical background in robotics or computing systems. Users should be familiar with basic networking, Linux terminal commands, and 
standard safety procedures when working with electronic and electromechanical systems.
